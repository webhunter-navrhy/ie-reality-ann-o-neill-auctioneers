Description

Ann O'Neill Auctioneers are delighted to present No. 13 Lakeview Drive to the market.

Ideally positioned within the popular and well-maintained Lakeside development, No. 13 Lakeview Drive is an attractive three-bedroom mid-terraced home, originally constructed circa 2005.

This warm and comfortable timber-frame property offers well-proportioned accommodation extending to approximately 85.75 m², making it an ideal choice for first-time buyers, families or investors.

The property enjoys a particularly convenient location, within walking distance of Kilkenny City Centre, McDonagh Junction Shopping Centre and Kilkenny Train Station, while the M9 motorway and all main routes are easily accessible.

Situated in a quiet cul-de-sac with minimal passing traffic, the property also benefits from private parking and a fenced/grassed rear garden.

Outside

Location

Lakeside is a popular residential development situated in an exceptionally convenient location close to the heart of Kilkenny City.

No. 13 Lakeview Drive is within easy walking distance of a wide range of amenities, including McDonagh Junction Shopping Centre, medical and leisure facilities, shops, restaurants and the city centre.

Kilkenny Bus and Train Stations are also within walking distance, providing excellent public transport connections. The property is ideally positioned for commuters, with easy access to the M9 motorway and all main routes.

Ideal Home or Investment

Whether seeking a comfortable family home, a first-time purchase or an investment opportunity, No. 13 Lakeview Drive offers an excellent combination of location, accommodation and convenience.

The property's turn-key presentation, B energy rating, manageable running costs and proximity to the city centre make this a particularly appealing opportunity.
